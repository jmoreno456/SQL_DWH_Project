# SQL Project

### Business Questions:
1. How has a team's performance changed from season to season?
2. Which teams have the best home records?
3. Which teams have the best attacking and defensive records?
4. How important is home advantage?
5. How does shot generation relate to match results?

### Information needed to answer business questions:
- team + seasons
- columns: season, team, matches, wins, draws, losses, goals_scored, goals_conceded, points, goal_difference
- goal_difference = goals_scored - goals_conceded
- count the total matches played
- sum total points
- most wins at home
- most goals scored
- least goals conceded
- total shots generated by each team in a match
- one row per team per season
